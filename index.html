Having trouble reading an image? ⌄

The standard scan automatically handles clearly dark or light backgrounds. If a layout differs or a saved crop misses text, tap Crop to include the entire question, then re-extract. Contrast and rotation are optional.

Still seeing mistakes? Try three alternative recognition settings, including opposite text/background colors. Your original text will be preserved unless you choose to replace it.
